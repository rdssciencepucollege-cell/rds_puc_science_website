The Visionary Founder

IN MEMORIAM

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."

Shri Tammanna Parshi founded RDS College with a singular vision: to bring high-quality, disciplined education to every student, regardless of their background. His belief that 'discipline is the foundation of character' remains our guiding light.

Today, we carry forward his legacy, ensuring that the flame of knowledge he ignited continues to burn brighter with each passing year, illuminating the paths of thousands of young minds.
